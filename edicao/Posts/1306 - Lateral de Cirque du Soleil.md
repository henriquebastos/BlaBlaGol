Opa, mancada, acabei de consultar as regras. Em inglês chamam isso de “handspring throw-in”. E é considerado válido mesmo.

“The handspring throw in is a type of throw-in where the player completes a front handspring while still holding onto the ball. This is used for long throw ins and for throw ins used a lot alike to a corner kick. This type of throw in follows all of the rules that the player must have both feet on the ground when he/she is releasing the ball, the ball is being thrown from behind the head, and the ball is being thrown with equal force by both hands. Strong abdominal muscles are required for this throw in.”
